# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Hackathon judges and anyone they invite, on a laptop or phone, with about five minutes. They arrive curious and slightly skeptical ("is this real?"). They want to paste two public links, watch AI agents date on that person's behalf, and read a ranking they can trust. A second audience is the video viewer, who sees the profile pages first, then the dating, then the rankings, in under three minutes.

## Product Purpose
Every person is represented by an agent that reads only their public LinkedIn and Instagram, builds an evidence-cited profile, then dates every other agent. Rankings come from the dates, not the profiles. Success: a judge understands in seconds what the agents saw, can click any claim to its evidence, can read any date transcript, and believes the ranking is explained, not magic.

## Brand Personality
Calm, warm, exact. A research lab that has the warmth of a good dating profile: Hinge's human, light, prompt-and-answer feel, minus the romance clichés. Voice is plain and specific. It states what the agents saw and how sure it is, and says plainly what it cannot know ("we rank compatibility, not eligibility").

## Anti-references
- Generic dating apps: pink, hearts, swipe cards, flame icons.
- Purple and blue AI gradients, glowing orbs, chat-bubble clichés.
- Cold corporate dashboards: gray KPI tiles, blue buttons, chart junk.
- Emoji decoration and cute copy ("find your spark").

## Design Principles
1. **Show the evidence.** Every claim on a profile expands to the observation and the post or job behind it. Confidence is always visible.
2. **Transcripts are the product.** Reading a date must feel like reading a real conversation: good measure, speaker clarity, nothing competing.
3. **Say what the system cannot know.** Honest limits, low-confidence flags and "not enough signal" states are first-class UI, never hidden.
4. **Warm, not romantic.** Human faces of the data (names, quotes, voices) over abstractions; no hearts, no gamification.
5. **Never a bare spinner.** Every wait names what is happening and what failed, when something fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard operation, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, works at phone width. No special requirements beyond that.
